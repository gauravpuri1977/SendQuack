Send emails from a spreadsheet

If your recipient list is already in a spreadsheet, manually sending one email at a time creates unnecessary work. SendQuack lets you use an Excel file as the contact source so you can prepare one message and personalize it for each recipient.

The current workflow is intentionally simple: your spreadsheet supplies the recipient Name and Email Address, while SendQuack handles the email composition, personalization and preview in the browser.

Why send from a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets are already a natural place to maintain small contact lists, event attendees, customer follow-ups and other structured recipient data. Using the spreadsheet directly avoids re-entering those contacts into another system before you can communicate with them.

  • Less repetitive work: write the message once rather than editing individual copies.
  • More personal communication: use the recipient's name instead of sending an obviously generic message.
  • Fewer copy-and-paste mistakes: the contact data comes from the uploaded file.
  • Clear review step: preview the message before sending.

Excel today, simple workflow throughout
