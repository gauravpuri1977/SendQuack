Anti-Spam Policy

Send responsibly.
No spam. No exceptions.

SendQuack is designed for legitimate communication with people you are permitted to contact. We do not allow our service to be used for unsolicited or abusive messaging.

Updated 26 September 2026
Our position on spam

SendQuack must not be used to send unsolicited bulk messages, deceptive communications, phishing messages, or messages to unlawfully obtained contact lists.

1. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the contacts you upload to SendQuack and for ensuring that you have a lawful and appropriate basis to communicate with those recipients.

By using SendQuack, you confirm that your messages and use of recipient information comply with applicable laws, regulations and contractual obligations.

2. Permitted contact lists

You should only upload and contact recipients where you have an appropriate reason or permission to communicate with them. This may include customers, members, employees, event participants, subscribers or other contacts with whom you have a legitimate relationship.

3. Prohibited activities

SendQuack must not be used for unsolicited bulk messaging, phishing, impersonation, fraud, scams, deceptive messages, malware distribution, harassment or other unlawful or abusive communications.

You must not use purchased, harvested, scraped or otherwise unlawfully obtained email lists to send messages through SendQuack.

4. Sender identity

Users must not intentionally provide false or misleading sender information or attempt to conceal the identity or origin of a message.

5. Recipient choice

SendQuack includes an unsubscribe mechanism for outgoing email and maintains unsubscribe or suppression status to help prevent further sending where a recipient opts out. Senders must respect recipient preferences and must not remove, obscure, bypass or attempt to circumvent these protections.

6. Bounces and complaints

SendQuack may prevent further delivery to addresses that unsubscribe, generate permanent delivery failures, submit spam complaints or produce other signals indicating that further messages should not be sent.

These protections help safeguard recipients and maintain the reliability and reputation of the SendQuack service.

7. Abuse prevention

We may monitor service activity and delivery signals for the purpose of detecting spam, abuse, fraud, excessive complaints or other violations of this policy.

We may restrict, suspend or terminate access to SendQuack where we reasonably believe the service is being used in violation of this policy or in a way that may harm recipients, the service or its delivery infrastructure.

8. Reporting abuse

If you believe that a message sent using SendQuack is spam, fraudulent, abusive or otherwise violates this policy, please contact:

abuse@sendquack.com

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Anti-Spam Policy as SendQuack evolves, including as we introduce additional communication channels or abuse-prevention capabilities.

10. Effective date

This policy is effective from 26 September 2026.
