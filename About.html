Simple communication.

SendQuack is built around a simple idea: sending personalized messages to your contacts shouldn't require complicated marketing software.

What is SendQuack?

SendQuack is a simple communication tool that helps users send personalized messages to multiple contacts using information they already have in a spreadsheet.

Upload your contacts, compose your message once, personalize it using fields such as a recipient's name and salutation, preview the result, and send.

Upload. Personalize. Send.

That's the philosophy behind SendQuack: make everyday personalized communication straightforward and accessible.

Why we built it

Many people occasionally need to communicate with a group of customers, members, colleagues, residents, participants or other contacts without needing a full-scale email marketing platform.

SendQuack focuses on that simpler use case: start with a spreadsheet and get your personalized communication sent with as little friction as possible.

Responsible communication

Simplicity should not come at the expense of responsible use. SendQuack is intended for legitimate communication with recipients whom users are permitted to contact.

We do not support spam, phishing, deceptive messaging, purchased or unlawfully obtained contact lists, or other abusive uses of the service.

Where we're going

SendQuack currently focuses on personalized email. Our goal is to bring the same straightforward experience to additional communication channels, including WhatsApp and SMS, while keeping the product simple and easy to use.
