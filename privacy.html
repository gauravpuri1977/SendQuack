Privacy Policy

Your data matters.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SendQuack processes, why we process it, and how information is handled when you use our service.

Updated 26 September 2026
Our approach

SendQuack is designed to make personalized communication simple while treating user and recipient information responsibly.

1. Information we process

When you use SendQuack, we may process information that you provide directly to the service. This can include sender information, recipient names, salutations, email addresses, email subjects and message content.

Contact information may be supplied through spreadsheets uploaded or selected by users for the purpose of preparing personalized communications.

2. How we use information

Information submitted to SendQuack may be used to personalize messages, deliver communications requested by the user, display previews, record delivery activity, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse and operate the service.

3. Recipient information

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate and lawful basis for providing recipient information to SendQuack and for communicating with those recipients.

Recipient information should not be uploaded to SendQuack where the user is not permitted to process or use that information for the intended communication.

4. Message and delivery information

SendQuack may retain information relating to messages sent through the service. This can include the recipient email address, sender information, reply-to address, subject, message content, sending time, delivery status and identifiers associated with message delivery.

Delivery events such as successful delivery, permanent bounces and recipient complaints may also be processed so that SendQuack can track message status and protect its delivery infrastructure.

5. Suppression information

SendQuack may maintain suppression records for email addresses associated with unsubscribes, permanent delivery failures, recipient complaints or other circumstances where additional messages should not be sent.

Suppression information may be used to prevent future delivery attempts and help protect recipients and the reliability of the service.

6. Service providers

SendQuack uses third-party technology and infrastructure providers to operate the service. Information may therefore be processed by service providers where necessary to host the application, store service information and deliver messages.

These providers currently include Amazon Web Services for email delivery, Render for application hosting, Supabase for database infrastructure, and GitHub Pages for hosting the SendQuack web interface.

7. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and operate SendQuack, maintain message and delivery records, prevent abuse, resolve technical problems and meet applicable legal obligations.

We may update our retention practices as the service evolves and as additional account and data-management capabilities are introduced.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through SendQuack. However, no internet-based service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Users are also responsible for protecting access to their devices, accounts and information used with SendQuack.

9. Spam and abusive use

SendQuack may process message activity, bounce information and complaint information to identify and prevent spam, fraudulent activity and other misuse of the service.

Please see our Anti-Spam Policy for additional information.

10. Your privacy enquiries

Questions or requests concerning privacy or personal information can be directed to:

privacy@sendquack.com

11. Children's privacy

SendQuack is not intended to be used by children to operate messaging campaigns or manage recipient contact lists. Users should not knowingly provide children's personal information to the service unless they have an appropriate lawful basis to do so.

12. International processing

The technology providers used to operate SendQuack may process or store information in countries other than the country in which a user or recipient is located.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SendQuack evolves, including when new features, communication channels or service providers are introduced.

Material changes will be reflected by updating this page and its effective date.

14. Effective date

This Privacy Policy is effective from 26 September 2026.
